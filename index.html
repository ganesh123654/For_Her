01

The Day I First Saw You

09 February 2025

Ninnu first time choosindi 09/02/2025... Bus stand lo Ammavari temple ki velthunapudu, oka frock vesukuni mee iddari friends tho vachav.

Nenu bus ekke mundu ninnu first time chusanu. Tarvatha bus lo driver sudden ga brake veyyadam tho nuvvu accidentally naa meeda padav. ❤️

Akkade manam actual ga kalisam, and together Ammavari darshanam complete chesam.

Appudu adi oka normal day la anipinchina, ippudu aa roju gurthochina prathi sari naaku automatic ga chala happy ga, special ga anipisthundi. ❤️

Appudu naaku teliyadu... aa chinna journey mana story ki beginning avuthundani.

💗
02

When You Became Special

11 February 2025

First lo nuvvu naa Instagram adigav, and nenu naa phone number icha. Ala mana conversations start ayyayi.

Ninnu first lo chusinappudu entha mudduga and chinna pillala anipinchavo... na manasuki chala baga nachav.

ninnu oka special name tho pilavali ani anipinchi... ninnu "NANA" ani pilavadam start chesa. ❤️

Nenu nee pakkana unnappudu chala special ga feel ayyevadini. Adi enduko appudu kuda complete ga explain cheyyalekapoyevadini.

Manam kalisina week kuda complete avvakunda nuvvu naaku "I Love You" cheppav.

Literally, how can I say no to you? Nee small cutie face valla naa heart already chala special ga feel avuthunte... ela no cheppagalanu? ❤️

Ala mana conversations continue avuthu, days pass avuthu, mana madhya love kuda roju roju ki inka ekkuva avuthu vachindi.

03

The Little Things

First lo naa academic schedule chala busy ga undedi. Morning early 4 ki lechi, 7:30 ki college ki velli, afternoon vachaka padukunevadini.

Appudu nuvvu naaku calls chesedanivi. Naa mobile silent lo undedi, kani nuvvu chala missed calls ichedanivi.

Nenu lechaka naa mobile check chesinappudu nee missed calls anni chusi chala special ga feel ayyevadini. ❤️

Natho matladali ane curiosity anukovala, leka nannu miss ayyav anukovala... naaku teliyadu. Kani aa missed calls chusinappudu naaku matram chala special ga anipinchedi.

Some moments are unforgettable memories. Appudu avi chinna moments la anipinchina, ippudu avi gurthosthe chala special ga untayi.

Nenu always first argue chesevadini... because I love you most. ❤️

Oka roju manam full night call lo unnam. Truth or dare aadukunnam, konni naughty things kuda chesam, and without sleep manam iddaram okariki okaram company ichukunnam.

Aa roju naaku ippatiki gurthundi. Aa night ni entha years ayina nenu marchipolenu. 🌙❤️

Our Memories

16 February 2025

Idi mana second meet. ❤️

Niku practical ayindi, and nenu ninnu chusina appudu white leggin lo chala shining ga unnav.

Akkada meet ayyam, tarvatha Ammavari temple ki vellam. Akkadi nundi Raghavaya Park ki vellam.

Aa roju lo jarigina prathi chinna moment, prathi place, prathi feeling... ippatiki na heart lo alane undi. ❤️

And This Is Only The Beginning

Aa tharvatha manam chala sarlu kalusukunam. Prathi meet tho inka enno memories create ayyayi.

Ila chala memories, chala fights, chala arguments... kani avi anni mana story lo oka part ayipoyayi.

Nenu arichina, kopam padina, happy ga unna, sad ga unna... naa prathi emotion lo nuvvu untav. ❤️

Naa prathi alochana lo nuvvu untav. Naa life lo jarige prathi feeling lo somehow nuvvu untav.

Mana story lo enni fights unna, enni arguments unna, enni days distance lo unna... I still choose you. ❤️
